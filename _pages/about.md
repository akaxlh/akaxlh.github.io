<p style="text-align:justify">I am a professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen). I was a research assistant professor at The University of Hong Kong. I received my Ph.D. (2021) and Bachelor's (2017) from South China University of Technology.</p>

<p style="text-align:justify">My research focuses on LLM agents, AI-Generated Content (AIGC), graph foundation models, retrieval-augmented generation, and recommendation systems.</p>
